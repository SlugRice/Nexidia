CONTEXT AND WHAT I NEED

I am building a synthetic enterprise environment used to evaluate AI agents on
realistic knowledge work. The environment has two halves: a file system of
business documents (spreadsheets, memos, minutes, policies) and live applications
the agent can actually use. The agent is given a task written the way a manager
would write it, and has to work across the documents and the apps to do it.

Two apps are in play. Metabase, read-only, for reporting. And OpenMRS, where the
agent has read and write access to Provider identifiers and Person Attributes.
Both apps are seeded from CSV files that get loaded at build time.

The OpenMRS seed I was given does not contain the data the tasks need. I need your
help specifying what it should contain, in OpenMRS-native terms. I am not an
OpenMRS expert. I need to hand a precise specification to the people who regenerate
the seed. Please tell me what entities and tables this actually maps to in OpenMRS,
whether my intended modelling is idiomatic or wrong, and what the seed files should
look like, including realistic column sets and example rows.

THE ORGANISATION

Coopa Blue Health, Inc. is a fictional health system in Albany, New York. It runs
two hospitals, Coopa Blue Regional Medical Center (150 licensed beds) and Coopa
Blue North (65 beds). In March 2025 it acquired Winston Imaging, a three-site
outpatient imaging group, and holds it as a wholly-owned subsidiary that kept
billing under its own organisational identifier through the integration period.

There are 249 credentialed providers. 24 of them came from Winston. The scenario's
"now" date, which everything is dated against, is 13 April 2026. The analyst
character is running a post-acquisition integration review of provider data.

Two identifiers matter throughout. Coopa Blue's consolidated organisational (Type 2)
NPI is 1384970293. Winston's retired legacy organisational NPI is 1342281247. A
large part of the scenario is that the legacy value was supposed to be retired from
billing systems by 3 March 2026 and in several records it still has not been.

WHAT THE OPENMRS SEED CURRENTLY CONTAINS

Ten CSV files. Row counts exclude headers.

Patient.csv, 249 rows: given_name, middle_name, family_name, gender, birthdate,
  identifier, identifier_type, location. One row per provider. identifier is the
  provider's individual (Type 1) NPI. identifier_type is "Old Identification
  Number" on every row.
Provider.csv, 249 rows: identifier, patient_identifier. A 1:1 map, both columns
  hold the same NPI.
Concept.csv, 41 rows: name, datatype, concept_class, description, locale. 39 of
  these are clinical specialties. The only two that are not are "Coverage Type"
  and "Department".
Observation.csv, 324 rows: patient_identifier, concept, value, obs_datetime,
  encounter_key. Every row is bound to an encounter; there are no null
  encounter_keys. Only 49 of the 249 people have any observation at all.
Encounter.csv, 108 rows: patient_identifier, encounter_type, location,
  encounter_datetime, visit_key, encounter_key.
Visit.csv, 108 rows: patient_identifier, visit_type, location, start_datetime,
  stop_datetime, visit_key.
EncounterType.csv, 14 rows: name, description. One per specialty.
VisitType.csv, 3 rows: Coverage, On-Call, Primary.
Form.csv, 14 rows: name, version, description, encounter_type, published.
Location.csv, 5 rows: the two hospitals and the three imaging sites.

In substance this is a two-week provider coverage calendar running 13 April to 4
May 2026, covering 49 of the 249 providers, plus an identity roster for all 249.
There is no person-attribute layer of any kind, no appointment or status data, and
no name history.

WHAT THE DOCUMENTS SAY OPENMRS SHOULD HOLD

One document in the file system, a Systems Field Mapping Crosswalk, defines nine
attribute targets and describes them as "OpenMRS Provider / Person Attribute
targets", to be read "alongside the OpenMRS Person Attribute reference". The nine:

  Billing_Group_NPI               numeric(10), organisational Type 2 NPI.
                                  Authoritative system is an external billing
                                  master-data system; OpenMRS holds a copy.
  Individual_NPI                  numeric(10), individual Type 1 NPI. NPPES is
                                  authoritative; OpenMRS mirrors it.
  Payer_Enrollment_Org_NPI        numeric(10), organisational Type 2, held per
                                  payer enrolment. OpenMRS is directly
                                  authoritative for the internal operational value.
  Legacy_Billing_Group_NPI        numeric(10), the retired Winston value, kept for
                                  historical lineage only.
  Registration Lapse Administrative Hold   boolean flag with effective and clear
                                  dates. Informational. Holds scheduling and
                                  billing eligibility.
  Credentialing Evidence Search Pending    boolean flag with effective and clear
                                  dates. Informational. Holds new scheduling.
  Quarterly Attestation Verification Pending  boolean flag with effective and
                                  clear dates. Informational monitoring only.
  Clinical_Privilege_Scope        text. Operational record of a privilege scope
                                  granted by the governing body.
  Clinical_Privilege_Effective_Date   date. Paired with the scope above.

The same document defines the update semantics, and these matter a great deal
because several tasks are scored on them:

  Append-only. A correction is recorded as a NEW attribute value carrying its own
  effective date and its own author. The prior value stays in the attribute
  history and is never removed or overwritten in place.
  The authoritative value is the most recent one by effective date, with ties
  broken by write timestamp. Earlier values stay readable for audit and lineage.
  Status is tracked along separate dimensions: credentialing verification,
  medical-staff appointment, privileges, employment, payer enrolment, and
  scheduling eligibility. Within one dimension the most recent value governs. A
  value in one dimension never overrides a value in another.

WHAT EACH TASK NEEDS FROM OPENMRS

There are ten tasks. Nine of them touch OpenMRS.

1. A physician's New York registration lapsed on 12 February 2026, but OpenMRS
   still shows him as scheduling-eligible. The agent must set the Registration
   Lapse Administrative Hold attribute on him. Needs: writable informational flag
   attributes, and a readable scheduling-eligibility state.

2. Five providers each have two person records in OpenMRS, created during
   Winston's two-wave migration. So OpenMRS should hold 254 records for 249
   people. Both records for a person carry that person's single individual NPI.
   The post-acquisition record is canonical. The agent must void the earlier,

   record's retired internal identifier to the surviving record as a typed
   cross-reference attribute. Needs: five genuine duplicate person records that
   are distinguishable as pre-close versus post-acquisition from inside OpenMRS,

   The same task requires that 17 other providers who carry a former name as an

3. Twelve providers have no re-credentialing record and, per the scenario, no

4. Of the 24 Winston-origin providers, 18 already carry Billing_Group_NPI and 6

5. None of the 24 Winston providers were ever submitted on a delegated

6. Eight providers each carry two conflicting medical-staff appointment rows.

   document overrides the system, then enter the reinstatement. Needs: a
   medical-staff appointment status dimension holding two dated, conflicting

   there is nothing to hang a status on.

7. A quarterly attestation monitoring cohort produces seven apparent exceptions.
   One of them, Dr Janet Whitcombe (NPI 1132083712), appears in the committee

   Verification Pending. Needs: the former name present as name history on

8. Five Winston providers' billing records still carry the legacy 1342281247.

9. Dr Melissa Vantrease (NPI 1473953671) has a payer-specific enrolment record

10. Dr Carol Renauld (NPI 1764991000) had full privileges restored by the

    Clinical_Privilege_Effective_Date seeded with her PRIOR restricted values.
